Karunya Arogya Suraksha Padhathi
Date: 15 June 2025
Project Name: Karunya Health Security Scheme (KASP), Karunya Benevolent Fund (KBF),
Arogya Kiranam.
Assistance/Services Received: Free treatment is provided to the public through government
and private hospitals empaneled under the scheme.
Karunya Arogya Suraksha Yojana (KASP) - Free medical assistance of Rs. 5 lakh per family per
financial year
Karunya Benevolent Fund (KBF) - Free medical assistance of Rs. 2 lakhs to the family (Medical
assistance of up to Rs. 3 lakhs for kidney/kidney related diseases)
Arogya Kiranam - Free treatment assistance in government hospitals for children under 18
years of age
Eligibility Criteria
Karunya Arogya Suraksha Padhathi (KASP): Anyone with a valid RSBY/CHIS card for the
period 2018-2019, are eligible to join the new scheme.
In addition to the RSBY/CHIS categories, weaker sections identified by the Central
Government as per the 2011 Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) are also eligible for
inclusion in the scheme.
Families that have received a direct letter from the Central Government are eligible for
inclusion in the scheme.
Karunya Benevolent Fund (KBF): Families with an annual income of less than ₹3 lakh
are eligible for inclusion in the Karunya Benevolent Fund (KBF), provided they are not
eligible for benefits under the Karunya Arogya Suraksha Padhathi (KASP).

Arogyakiranam: For children aged between one year and below 18 years, eligibility
applies if their parents are not central or state government employees or income tax
payers.
Treatment for children up to one year of age is covered under the JSSK scheme.
How to Apply
Applications can be submitted directly at the Karunya and Arogya Suraksha Scheme kiosks
available in government and private hospitals empanelled under the scheme.
Address to Apply: At the Karunya Arogya Suraksha Padhathi kiosks in government-private
hospitals empanelled under the scheme.
Deadline: No deadline has been specified.
